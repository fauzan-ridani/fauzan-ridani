## 👋 About Me

I'm a developer focused on **automation** and **AI integration** — turning repetitive, manual work into scripts and systems that run on their own. From scraping hundreds of visual assets with Selenium, to modifying the Android environment through Termux, to wiring a Discord bot into the Gemini API, I'd rather solve a real problem than just collect tools.

Most of what I know comes from hands-on experimentation — trying, breaking, and fixing until a script actually holds up. I care less about listing technologies and more about whether a project does something useful.
